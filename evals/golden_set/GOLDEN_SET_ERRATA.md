# Golden set errata (known defects, NOT applied)

`golden_set.jsonl` is frozen (see `GOLDEN_SET_CHANGELOG.md`). My grades of the model's answers were made against these reference answers, so editing them would make the grades meaningless.

These five reference answers have known defects. They are recorded here instead of being edited. They would only be corrected in a deliberate new version (v3) after which **every** answer is graded again and the baseline is reset.

| Question | Defect in the frozen reference | Corrected reference (for v3) |
|---|---|---|
| N01 | Includes a consequence the question didn't ask about, so a correct answer ("ten seconds") was graded partial | Ten seconds. |
| G29 | Omits the second sending-off condition on page 123 | When the player denies the opposing team a goal or an obvious goal-scoring opportunity by (1) a deliberate handball offence (except a goalkeeper within their own penalty area) or (2) a non-deliberate handball offence outside their own penalty area. |
| G26 | Omits that a goalkeeper may not touch the ball after releasing it and before another player touches it (page 118) | A goalkeeper may not touch the ball with the hand/arm (a) after releasing it and before it has touched another player, or (b) after it has been deliberately kicked to them by a team-mate or received directly from a team-mate's throw-in, unless they have clearly kicked or attempted to kick it to release it into play. |
| G15 | Says "plus other listed cases", which is vague | When it has wholly passed over the goal line or touchline on the ground or in the air, when play has been stopped by the referee, or when it touches a match official, remains on the field and a team starts a promising attack, the ball goes directly into the goal, or the team in possession changes (in these match-official cases play restarts with a dropped ball). |
| G20 | Vague about the fifth VAR category; the full list is on page 155 | Five categories: (a) goal/no goal, (b) penalty/no penalty, (c) red card, including a clearly incorrect second caution, (d) mistaken identity, and (e) a clearly incorrectly awarded corner kick, which is a competition option that applies only if the decision can be changed immediately without delaying the restart. (Also add the phrase "categories of decision/incident which may be reviewed" as an alternative key phrase, pages 154, 155 and 185.) |

When reporting results, mention these defects next to any grade they affect (N01, G15, G20, G26, G29).
